## Notes for the next developer

- Photographs are `.webp` at the native resolution stored in the design file. Aspect ratios are unchanged and labels such as FEATURED POSTS / FASHION were not baked into the images.
- Decorative PNGs and logo rasters use transparent backgrounds so they can sit on white or gold page backgrounds.
- Prefer SVG for logos, dividers, and icons when scaling. Use the extracted `*-gold-outline.png` emblem when the glossy 3D hanger-butterfly look is needed.
- Gold decorative color is approximately `#D4AF37` / `#C5A47E`. Heading rose is approximately `#C4788A`.
